# FAQ

## Does accepted mean settled?

No. It means finite checks for the declared scope passed.

## Does PIC prove ASI?

No. ASI-proxy is a measurement term for verified capability formation under a
finite protocol and matched resources.

## Can check commands call providers?

No. Only explicit operation dispatch can call an adapter after preflight and
approval.

## Are missing values zero?

No. Required missing values are unknown and normally cause abstention.
