# Glossary

- **Capability packet:** checked, reusable data plus evidence and residuals.
- **Residual:** unresolved work that must remain visible.
- **Verifier:** an independent checker for a declared evidence scope.
- **Resource envelope:** wall time, tokens, calls, compute, review, and risk limits.
- **ASI-proxy:** protocol-relative measured capability formation, not real ASI.
- **Settled:** every obligation in the declared scope has been discharged.
- **Operation receipt:** evidence of a provider call, not evidence of its outcome.
